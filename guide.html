What this tool actually is, what it isn't, and how to get around it. Read the risk section before you build a slip.

⚠️ Risk & Disclosure — read this first

This is a data analysis and backtesting tool built from real 2024–2025 NFL play-by-play data. It is not a sportsbook, does not process real money, and nothing you do here places an actual bet.

  • Historical performance doesn't guarantee future results. Every hit rate on this site is a look backward — how a line would have performed against real games — not a prediction of what will happen next.
  • "Fair odds" have no vig removed and no sportsbook behind them. They're 1 ÷ historical hit rate, a math exercise, not a live market price.
  • The AI Assist feature is a rules-based heuristic, not a live model call or expert opinion. It swaps legs based on simple probability comparisons.
  • Rookie stats use a single season with no prior-year holdout, so their confidence numbers are less rigorous than veteran numbers — even though they're displayed the same way.
  • Bet tracking is a simulation. "Submitted" bets resolve instantly against real historical game logs (or a probability-weighted simulation where exact logs aren't available) — this shows how the engine would have performed, it does not mean any wager occurred.
  • If you use this alongside real sportsbook betting, remember: past stats are one input, not a system that guarantees profit. Bet only what you can afford to lose, and treat every number here as a starting point for your own judgment, not a final answer.

What this is

A stats dashboard covering NFL, WNBA, MLB, and College Football. Real historical data, tested against actual outcomes before anything is shown — not projections presented as certainty.

This is data, not advice. Everything here is historical statistics and backtested reference ranges — not a recommendation to bet, and not a guarantee of any outcome. Sports outcomes are inherently uncertain. If you choose to bet, only risk what you can afford to lose, and treat every number here as one input among many, not a final answer.

How to use it

DASHBOARD

The landing page. Quick stats, your top current picks, an upcoming slate grouped by date (click a game to jump to it), and your recent bet log.

OFFENSE / DEFENSE

Browse player season stats (Batters/Pitchers for MLB). Click a card for splits and recent games.

MARKET PULSE

Live prices from Kalshi and Polymarket — championship odds and season-long markets.

PROP FLOORS + PARLAY

Reference ranges for player stats, backtested against real games. Tap one to add it to a slip. If a real sportsbook line is connected it auto-fills; otherwise type in your own book's line to compare. Build a slip across players, then Copy Slip to paste into your sportsbook yourself, or Submit Bet to track it here.

MATCHUP

See how a player's numbers look against a hypothetical opponent. Not tied to an actual scheduled game — a "what if" tool.

TEAMS

Browse team offense/defense stats side by side, with rank. Click a team for its roster; search works here for any sport, including the 130+ College Football teams. A quick-search near the top of the page works from any tab.

TRACKING

Your submitted bet history and record. Import history from another sportsbook by pasting text — review before anything saves. Stored in your browser only.

COLLEGE FOOTBALL (needs its own free key)

Built differently since CFB books rarely offer player props — this sport focuses on spread, total, and moneyline instead, with a confidence read on each. No parlay slip for this sport. Requires a free key from collegefootballdata.com/key.

How to read the confidence badges

Every number is checked against real games before it's shown as Strong, Moderate, or Limited confidence — Limited means there isn't much real history behind that specific number yet, so treat it with extra caution. A TEAM CHANGE badge means the player's history includes time on a different team. A ROOKIE badge means there's no prior-season history at all.
