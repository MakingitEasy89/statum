Statum — Guide

What this tool actually is, what it isn't, and how to get around it. Read the risk section before you build a slip.

⚠️ Risk & Disclosure — read this first

This is a data analysis and backtesting tool built from real 2024–2025 NFL play-by-play data. It is not a sportsbook, does not process real money, and nothing you do here places an actual bet.

  • Historical performance doesn't guarantee future results. Every hit rate on this site is a look backward — how a line would have performed against real games — not a prediction of what will happen next.
  • "Fair odds" have no vig removed and no sportsbook behind them. They're 1 ÷ historical hit rate, a math exercise, not a live market price.
  • The AI Assist feature is a rules-based heuristic, not a live model call or expert opinion. It swaps legs based on simple probability comparisons.
  • Rookie stats use a single season with no prior-year holdout, so their confidence numbers are less rigorous than veteran numbers — even though they're displayed the same way.
  • Bet tracking is a simulation. "Submitted" bets resolve instantly against real historical game logs (or a probability-weighted simulation where exact logs aren't available) — this shows how the engine would have performed, it does not mean any wager occurred.
  • If you use this alongside real sportsbook betting, remember: past stats are one input, not a system that guarantees profit. Bet only what you can afford to lose, and treat every number here as a starting point for your own judgment, not a final answer.

What this is

A stats and analytics dashboard covering NFL, WNBA, and MLB — every skill-position player, QB, kicker, and pass rusher in the NFL; every WNBA player; every qualified MLB batter and pitcher. NFL stats are tagged with the real defensive front and coverage shell that was on the field for that play — not a static "this team runs a 4-3" label.

How to use it

DASHBOARD

The landing page. Real stat tiles (strong-confidence prop count, open bets, portfolio ROI), your top 5 strongest current picks, an upcoming slate grouped by real date (click any game to jump straight to props for those two teams), and your recent bet log. Nothing here is placeholder — every number is computed live from your actual data.

OFFENSE / DEFENSE

Browse every player's season stats (for MLB this is Batters/Pitchers). Click a card to see splits, recent games with real opponents, and — for NFL — every touchdown or sack with the exact front/coverage context it happened in.

MARKET PULSE

Live prices from Kalshi and Polymarket, blended together — championship odds, season-long leader races, and a GEX-style order book depth panel showing where real money is actually stacked. If a live fetch fails for any reason, it falls back to the last known snapshot rather than showing nothing.

PROP FLOORS + PARLAY

The core tool. Each player-stat gets three lines — P25, P50, P75 — backtested out-of-sample against real games. Tap a tranche to add it to your slip. If you've connected a real sportsbook odds feed (see below), matching lines auto-fill with a green "✓ Real line found" indicator; otherwise use "📏 Compare to your sportsbook line" and type in what you see on your own book to get an instant Strong Over/Moderate Over/Neutral/Moderate Under/Strong Under read.

Combined probability accounts for real same-team correlation where enough shared game history exists — not just naive multiplication — with a note showing when and how much the adjustment moved the number. Use ✨ AI Assist to get a suggested swap for your weakest leg, 📋 Copy Slip to format everything for pasting into DraftKings/FanDuel yourself, or 🎯 Submit Bet to resolve instantly and send it to Tracking.

MATCHUP

For each player in your slip, pick a hypothetical opponent and see real points/runs-allowed rank and (NFL only) scheme signature. Works across all three sports in one mixed slip. Treat it as a "what if" tool — it isn't locked to an actual scheduled game.

TEAMS

Browse every team's real offense (points/runs scored) and defense (allowed) stats side by side, with rank. Click a team to see its full roster — click any player there to jump straight to their own detail page.

TRACKING

Your submitted bet history — wins, losses, net units, and which tranche has actually worked best for you. Click 🔍 Check vs a real game on any leg to independently verify it against an actual embedded game log, separate from however it originally resolved. You can also 📥 Import Bet History from another sportsbook — paste in whatever text you have, review the parsed rows (edit or delete anything wrong), and import. Everything here is stored in your browser only — it won't follow you to another device.

Other things worth knowing

☀️ / 🌙 Day & Night mode

Switches automatically by your local time, or click the toggle next to the sport switcher to override it manually.

⚙ "How this works" toggles

Methodology explanations are tucked behind small gear-icon toggles throughout the app instead of always taking up space — click one open any time you want the detail.

Real sportsbook lines (optional)

If a real odds API key is connected, prop cards auto-fill with actual DraftKings/FanDuel lines instead of needing manual entry. This is entirely optional — manual comparison works fine without it.

How the numbers are built

Every ladder line is a percentile of a player's prior-season game log, tested against real current-season games the line never saw — an out-of-sample backtest, not a threshold grading its own training data.

P25

25th percentile — a conservative floor, historically cleared ~75% of the time.

P50

Median — a 50/50 line.

P75

75th percentile — the stretch tranche, ~25% historical clear rate, bigger number when it hits.

A TEAM CHANGE badge means the baseline line was set on a different team/offense than the one being tested. A ROOKIE badge means there's no prior-season baseline at all. A 📊 confidence badge (Strong/Moderate/Limited) tells you how many real out-of-sample games back that specific hit rate — treat Limited numbers with real caution.

Keeping it running

If you're the one operating this dashboard: it auto-updates itself twice a day via GitHub Actions (see the workflow file in the repo). Games-played, prop ladders, and live odds all refresh automatically — no manual steps needed for normal operation. A manual run is only needed if you're changing the code itself.
